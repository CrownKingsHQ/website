Meet Logan

Charleston craftsman. Family man. Lifelong builder.

CrownKings is built around Logan Flynn’s hands-on construction experience, his pride in getting the details right, and his belief that professional service should be as dependable as the finished work.

01 The craftsman behind CrownKings

A lifetime around construction shaped the way Logan approaches every room.

Logan Flynn is a Charleston, South Carolina local who has spent most of his life around construction, building, and hands-on craftsmanship.

Over the years, he developed a deep appreciation for the details that make a home feel intentional. Crown molding, trim, and architectural finish work may appear subtle, but they have an enormous effect on the way a room feels.

CrownKings grew from that experience and from Logan’s belief that beautiful work should be paired with honest communication, reliability, and respect for the customer’s property.

02 Local roots

Charleston is home.

Logan has strong roots in the Charleston area and understands the character, architectural styles, and expectations that come with working in Lowcountry homes and commercial spaces.

His approach is personal and straightforward. He listens to the customer, studies the space, explains the work clearly, and treats the property with the same care he would expect in his own home.

Outside of work, Logan enjoys spending time with his family and his two boys, ages 13 and 9. He also enjoys hunting and fishing at the family farm in Cottageville.

That balance of craftsmanship, family, and time outdoors keeps the business grounded in something simple: do honest work, take pride in it, and leave every space better than you found it.
