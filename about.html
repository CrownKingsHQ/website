Meet Logan

Charleston craftsman. Family man. Lifelong builder.

CrownKings is built around Logan Flynn’s hands-on construction experience, his respect for every customer and property, and his belief that the smallest details often make the greatest difference.

01 The craftsman behind CrownKings

More than 25 years of experience, focused into one specialist craft.

Logan Flynn is a Charleston, South Carolina local with more than 25 years of construction experience and a lifelong connection to building, problem-solving, and hands-on craftsmanship.

Throughout his career, he developed a deep appreciation for the architectural details that make a room feel balanced and complete. Crown molding may be subtle, but the right profile, scale, and finish can transform how an entire interior feels.

CrownKings grew from Logan’s decision to focus that experience on one specialty and pair exceptional workmanship with clear communication, dependable service, and genuine respect for every customer’s property.

02 Local roots

Charleston is home.

Logan understands the character, architecture, and expectations that come with working in Lowcountry homes and commercial properties.

His approach is personal and straightforward. He listens carefully, studies the space, explains the work clearly, and treats every property with the same care he would expect in his own home.

Away from the job site, Logan enjoys spending time with his family and his two boys, ages 13 and 8. He also enjoys hunting and fishing on his hunting property in Ridgeville.

That balance of family, craftsmanship, and time outdoors keeps the business grounded in a simple philosophy: do honest work, take pride in the details, and leave every space better than you found it.
